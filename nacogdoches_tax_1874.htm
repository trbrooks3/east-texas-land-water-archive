The supplied PDF contains a research copy of the assessment-roll page(s) consulted for the Upshaw family research. The PDF's extracted text is imperfect and should not be treated as an authoritative transcription; consult the original image for names and figures.

Scope and description

Assessment roll listing taxable property and associated valuations in Nacogdoches County for 1874. This is a genuine historical source, not a Tolliver County or New Providence record. Entries for people named Upshaw appear in the consulted pages; names, acreage and assessments require verification against the original image before quotation.
