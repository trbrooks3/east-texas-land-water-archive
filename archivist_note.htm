Note from the Archivist

The East Texas Land & Water Archive is a fictional archival project.

Tolliver County, its associated communities, and the records attributed to them are fictional. References to actual Texas counties, geographical features, and historical circumstances are included for context.

Any authentic historical documents reproduced in the archive are identified by their original sources.

The collection is intended for literary and educational purposes.
