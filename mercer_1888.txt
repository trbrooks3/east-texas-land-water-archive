FIELD BOOK OF H. A. MERCER
County Surveyor, Tolliver County, Texas
September–November 1888

Friday, September 21

Mr. Bell from New Providence came into the county office this morning. He has been having trouble with Mr. Edmund Lott over the road between their places. Wants the land surveyed again to show the road is his.

Was given the work and told to look over the old county measurements.

Told Bell I would get out there next week. Have some work yet to finish for the Smith family over in Nacogdoches County.

Monday, September 24

Out to the Smith place this morning.

Began at post oak marked S on north line.

East 672 varas to pine with two chops.

South 336 varas to stake near branch.

Good timber on the east side. Had a hard time getting the chain through the brush.

Will finish tomorrow.

Tuesday, September 25

Back out to Smith's.

From stake near branch:

South 336 varas to sweet gum.

West 672 varas to stake. Pine bears N. 30° W., 8 varas.

North 672 varas to beginning.

80 acres, more or less.

Stayed for dinner with the family. Four children. Came down from Kentucky last year. Good folks.

Wednesday, September 26

Finished the notes for the Smith deed and turned them in.

Started looking through Bell's papers after supper. Several surveys of the land around there and not much agreement between them.

Will take it up again tomorrow.

Thursday, September 27

Spent the morning on the Bell and Lott papers.

Lott patent No. 873, Vol. 19.

1,344 varas each side. Eastern corner at the west side of the creek bottom.

Hollis went out there in 1856. Has the old wagon road at 640 varas along the south line and 604 varas from there to the upper bank of the creek.

Can't find the sheet he refers to in his notes.

Spoke with the clerk after dinner. He agreed to come out with me tomorrow and help with the chain.

Might be that the old instruments were no good.

Friday, September 28

Out to Bell's this morning. Stopped at the house to let him know we would be down on the road.

He says Lott and his men have been down at the creek a good deal lately. Asked what they were doing. He didn't know.

Started where the old road crosses the south line of the Lott place.

To branch — 217 varas.

To remains of old pine — 393 varas.

To upper bank — 590¾ varas.

Ran the last distance again. Same within a few inches.

Some washing along the bank but not enough that I can see to make up the difference.

Don't make much sense of it yet. Bell won't be pleased.

Monday, October 1

Spent most of the day tracing the Bell and Lott deeds.

The Lotts have been buying land around here since the days of the Republic. A good deal of Yankee money in that family.

Had to go through the old Nacogdoches County papers for some of it.

Read more of Hollis's notes. Had trouble with his compass down by the creek. Says something about the stars going out one night. Might have had too much to drink.

One of his men thought there might be iron down in the creek bed. Could be.

Will have another look when I get out there.

Tuesday, October 2

Taken on some work over in Sabine County. Gentleman buying land for a cotton place. Several men hired to help with the work.

Bell will have to wait another week.

Wednesday, October 3

Got to the Sabine place before dinner.

Began at pine with three chops on west line.

North 1,344 varas to stake in open timber.

Good cotton ground once cleared. Lower part will need ditching.

Thursday, October 4

East 1,344 varas to red oak near head of branch.

South 1,344 varas to stake in pine timber.

Slow going through the brush. One of the men broke his axe handle. Lost a good part of the morning over it.

Friday, October 5

West 1,344 varas to beginning.

320 acres, more or less.

Marked off the ground the owner wants cleared. Says he wants forty acres ready by spring.

Finished up and headed home.

Tuesday, October 9

Out to New Providence to lay off a piece of ground for a Methodist meetinghouse.

Began at stake by road.

North 48 varas.

East 48 varas.

South 48 varas.

West 48 varas to beginning.

Room enough for the house and a yard.

Don't suppose the Baptists will be too pleased.

Stopped at Bell's store and told him what I found in the old papers. He wasn't pleased but didn't seem much surprised either.

Told him I would be back Thursday.

Going to Nacogdoches with the wife tomorrow. Her folks have been poorly.

Thursday, October 11

Stopped to see Bell this morning. Wants the road settled before winter.

Went down to the creek.

Lott has put up some sort of machine on the stonework down there. Wires running from it to a covered box. Some other instruments under a shelter.

Don't know what he means to do with it. Nothing I have ever needed for surveying.

Started at the same mark as on the 28th.

To branch — 217 varas.

To remains of old pine — 393 varas.

To upper bank — 604 varas.

Went back to the beginning and checked the mark. Right place.

Ran the distance again.

604 varas.

Looked over the chain. Nothing wrong with it that I could find.

Had some trouble with the compass down by the creek. Needle took a long time to settle. Thought it might be something in Lott's machine and moved farther off. Still had trouble with it.

Worked well enough up on the road.

Hasn't been enough rain since the 28th to move that bank so far.

Left a little after two.

Will speak to the clerk tomorrow.

Friday, October 12

Went over the figures with the clerk.

He remembers the distance we took on the 28th. Says he is sure we didn't miss a length of chain.

Yesterday's figure is the same as Hollis's.

Don't know what to make of it.

Monday, October 15

Heard this morning that Bell's house and store burned Saturday night. Nobody seems to know where he or his wife is.

Some talk in town about the Lotts. Can't get a straight account of what happened.

Best leave the road alone for now.

Thursday, October 18

Looked through Bell's deeds again.

Nothing there that gives him a clear right to the road. Nothing much in the Lott papers either.

Wish I had asked Bell where he got the notion the road was his. He seemed mighty sure of it.

Monday, October 22

Checked the chain against a known distance near the office.

No trouble with it.

Compass works well enough here too.

Will borrow another compass next time I go out to the creek.

Friday, October 26

Went over the figures again this afternoon.

Hollis — 604 varas.

Sept. 28 — 590¾ varas.

Oct. 11 — 604 varas.

Difference of 13¼ varas. Near enough 37 feet.

Would ordinarily take the last measure to be the right one since it agrees with Hollis. Trouble is the clerk was with me on the 28th and we ran that distance twice. He is as sure of the figure as I am.

The branch and the old pine came out the same both times. If we made a mistake it must have been between the pine and the bank.

Could have taken the wrong part of the bank for the end of the line. Don't believe I did, but can't swear to it without going back.

There has been some washing down there over the years. No telling how much since Hollis took his measure. Just because I got his figure on the 11th doesn't mean the bank is where it was in '56.

Can't throw out the first measure on account of the second, and can't give either man the road on the strength of what I have.

Will have to run it again with another chain and another man.

Not much use in putting a new line on paper until then.

Monday, November 5

Word from Justice Harwood. Wants to speak to me about Bell.

Got the field notes together and made copies of the old figures.

Don't know what I can tell him about the fire. Last saw Bell the morning of the 11th.

Still haven't settled the road.

Wednesday, November 7

Went before Justice Harwood and gave my statement.

Asked whether the road belonged to Bell or Lott. Told him I couldn't say.

Will need another examination of the ground before I can finish the work.

H. A. Mercer
County Surveyor
