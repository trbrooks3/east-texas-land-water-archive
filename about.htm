About the Archive

The East Texas Land & Water Archive is a fictional archival website created for a literary project. Tolliver County, New Providence, and their associated historical documents are invented. Other Texas counties named here are real, but their pages do not claim custody of records.

Any authentic historical sources added to this website will be identified and attributed separately from fictional transcriptions.
