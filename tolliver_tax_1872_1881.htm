Editorial note

Selected assessment entries for Tolliver County, prepared in the format of county tax rolls. Missing years or persons do not establish absence from the county. The Hargrove designation refers to the original land survey. The 1875 Grimes and 1880 Bell entries contain unreadable values; the 1881 Bell acreage and land valuation remain uncertain.
