Old North Church Minute Book — Historical Reference

External source: East Texas Research Center digital collection: Old North Church minute book.

The genuine nineteenth-century church minutes are a source for documentary conventions, including membership admissions, letters of dismissal, conference business, and clerk signatures. They are not New Providence Baptist Church records. The fictional 1885–1887 minutes are an original creative document.

Source images remain with the holding institution and are not reproduced here.
