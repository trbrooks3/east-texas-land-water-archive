Scope of the Archive

The East Texas Land & Water Archive provides access to selected documentary materials concerning land ownership, settlement, transportation, community institutions, and water-resource development in East Texas.

Records are arranged by county, collection, and document type. Materials include land patents, survey field books, county proceedings, correspondence, photographs, oral histories, and records relating to public works.

The archive is not comprehensive. Geographic and chronological coverage varies between collections, and the presence of a county or record group in the directory does not indicate that materials are available for every period.

Document descriptions retain the names, dates, locations, and measurements appearing in the available sources. Discrepancies between related records are not necessarily resolved in the accompanying notes. In some instances, the location of a recorded feature cannot be reconciled with subsequent surveys.

Transcriptions are supplied for reference purposes. Where available, identifying information and source descriptions accompany individual records. The absence of an original document or accompanying field sheet is noted when known.

Information concerning the preparation and status of the collection is provided in the Note from the Archivist.
