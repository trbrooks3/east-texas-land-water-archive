Old North Church Conference Minutes

County: Nacogdoches County, Texas
Record type: Baptist church conference minute book, selected manuscript images
Period represented: Nineteenth century (including 1859–1862 in the reviewed images)
Holding institution: East Texas Research Center, Stephen F. Austin State University
Source: East Texas Research Center digital item
Archive record: ETLWA-NAC-CHURCH-01

Document access

Manuscript image identifiers previously indexed here are available through the holding institution's digital collection linked above. Local research image copies are not hosted by this archive. Consult the holding institution's catalog for authoritative title, date span, and rights information.

Scope and description

Conference proceedings documenting membership, letters of dismissal, committee matters, discipline, and ordinary church business. These genuine records are distinct from the fictional New Providence Baptist Church minutes.
