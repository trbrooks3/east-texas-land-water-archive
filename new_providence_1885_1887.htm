1885

Saturday before the first Sabbath in February

The Church met after preaching by Elder [name uncertain].

The door was opened. None came forward.

Sister Caroline Lott being sick, Sisters Ruth Washington and Martha Whitfield were requested to visit her.

The brethren were requested to examine the meeting house.

Adjourned.

J. Whitfield, Clk.

April Conference

The committee reported several boards unsound on the north side of the house and the roof in need of attention.

Bro. H. Bell gave $1.00 toward repairs. Bro. Saml. Lott offered his labor. Bro. Parker to furnish nails.

Sister Caroline Lott reported better.

J. Whitfield

Saturday before the first Sabbath in June

The Church met in conference.

Bro. Andrew Hayes complained that Bro. William Carter had not returned a wagon tongue borrowed before planting.

Bro. Carter stated that it had been broken in use and that he intended to replace it.

On motion Bros. Samuel Lott and Thomas Coleman were requested to speak with the parties.

Adjourned.

J. Whitfield, Clerk

September

After preaching the Church met in conference.

Sister Mary Carter came forward and related her Christian experience and was received into fellowship.

The committee concerning the difficulty between Bros. Hayes and Carter being called upon, Bro. Coleman reported that the parties had not agreed.

Bro. Hayes reported the road to the meeting house in bad condition. The brethren agreed to work upon it Saturday next, weather permitting.

Collection 75 cts.

J. Whitfield

1886

Saturday before the first Sabbath in January

The Church met in conference.

Sisters Adeline Coleman and Louisa Parker were requested to assist with the younger children on the Sabbath.

Bro. Nathan Grimes requested the prayers of the Church, two of his children having been sick.

The Church united in prayer.

Adjourned.

J. Whitfield

March

No conference held on account of high water at the crossing.

J. W.

May Conference

After preaching the Church met.

Bro. Thomas Coleman reported that Elder [illegible] had agreed to preach on the third Sabbath in June if his expenses could be met.

Bro. Henry Bell gave $3.00. Bro. Elijah Freeman 50 cts. Sister Sarah Freeman to furnish dinner.

Bro. James Turner requested a letter of dismissal for his wife, who desired to unite with a church nearer her mother's residence.

Granted.

The minutes were read and approved.

J. Whitfield

[Entry without heading]

Sister Rebecca Bell reported that the quilt prepared for the Grimes family had been delivered.

The Church agreed that a collection be taken for slates for the children.

Bro. Wm. Carter was requested to inquire as to the price.

Bro. James Turner reported his wife's mother much improved.

No other business.

J. Whitfield

October

The Church met after divine service.

Bro. Carter reported the slates could be purchased in Nacogdoches for [amount illegible].

The purchase was postponed until after the cotton was gathered.

Sister Louisa Parker requested the use of the meeting house for the sisters on Thursday next.

Granted.

J. Whitfield, Clk.

1887

March Conference

The Church met in regular conference.

Bro. Benjamin Parker reported the floor near the entrance required new boards.

Bro. Henry Bell gave $5.00 toward lumber.

Sister Martha Whitfield reported that the widow of Bro. [name obscured] was in need of assistance.

A collection was ordered for her benefit.

Adjourned.

J. Whitfield

[One leaf missing between the March and August entries.]

August Conference

The Church met after preaching.

Bro. Saml. Lott reported the floor had been repaired.

Bro. Andrew Hayes was requested to see to the clearing of the burial ground.

Sister Ruth Washington requested assistance in making clothing for two children whose mother had lately died.

The sisters agreed to bring what they could spare on Thursday.

Bro. Whitfield being absent, Bro. Thomas Coleman acted as Clerk.

T. Coleman, Clk. pro tem.

September

The Church met in conference.

Bro. Parker called attention to the fence at the burial ground.

The matter was laid over.

Bro. Whitfield requested to be relieved of the duties of Clerk.

On motion Bro. Thomas Coleman was chosen Clerk for the remainder of the year.

T. Coleman

Saturday before the first Sabbath in November

After divine service the Church met in conference.

Bro. Thomas Coleman called attention to the roof over the west end of the meeting house, which had leaked during the last heavy rain.

Bro. Benjamin Parker reported that the shingles and boards wanted for the west end of the roof, together with nails, would cost near fifteen dollars, the brethren furnishing the labor.

The Church having insufficient funds, a motion was made to postpone the repairs until spring.

Bro. Henry Bell offered twelve dollars toward the materials if the brethren would undertake the work before Christmas.

Bros. Samuel Lott and Andrew Hayes agreed to assist.

The motion to postpone was withdrawn.

Bro. Parker was requested to purchase the materials.

Sister Rebecca Bell requested the prayers of the Church for a young woman lately come to reside with her family.

The minutes were read and adopted.

T. Coleman, Clerk

December Conference

The Church met after divine service.

Bro. Parker reported the repairs completed except one window shutter.

Bro. Bell stated that the account for materials was settled.

Sister Louisa Parker requested that the children of the Sabbath School be permitted to recite their lessons before the congregation on Christmas Day.

Granted.

No other business.

T. Coleman

Transcription Note

The entries reproduced here are selections from the surviving conference book of New Providence Baptist Church. They are not a continuous account of the congregation's meetings. Original abbreviations and variations in headings and signatures have been retained where legible. Bracketed text represents editorial notation. One leaf is missing between March and August 1887; other entries contain faded or uncertain readings. Matters appearing without a recorded conclusion should not be presumed to have remained unresolved. Decisions, settlements, and assistance rendered outside formal conference may not have been entered in the book.
