Technical Notes

The East Texas Land & Water Archive is a fictional archival project developed for literary and educational purposes.

Tolliver County, its associated communities, and the records attributed to them are fictional. References to actual Texas counties, geographical features, and historical circumstances are included for context.

Authentic historical documents, where reproduced, are identified by their original sources.

Project Development and AI Disclosure

This project was conceived, developed, and directed by Thomas R. Brooks III, Ph.D., in collaboration with ChatGPT (OpenAI), referred to throughout the development process as “Puck.”

Brooks is responsible for the project's original concept, narrative framework, historical research and verification, drafting and revision of archival documents, thematic development, editorial oversight, and final publication. His contributions include evaluating the historical accuracy of generated materials, developing original passages, refining documentary language, and establishing the project's narrative and historical continuity.

ChatGPT (Puck) contributes historical research assistance, preliminary document drafting, editorial suggestions, historical contextualization, archival organization, website development, HTML formatting, and technical support.

Both Brooks and ChatGPT participate in the iterative drafting and revision process. However, Brooks retains creative direction, exercises independent scholarly judgment in evaluating historical materials, and determines which content is approved for publication.

Responsibility for the final published content rests with Brooks.
